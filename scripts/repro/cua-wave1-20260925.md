W15/W16/W17/W18 constrain future reuse; semantic similarity cannot justify skipping capture. W20 constrains native lifetime during later batching.

One writer per shared file; #4052 and #3961 share runner paths, so combine only after scope/review. One mutable desktop session per fixture worker. Use existing functions/test seams/data records; extract a helper only for two demonstrated users with an agreed owner, not because the graph suggests a new service.

A packet may finish as evidence, a question, a patch, or an explicit no-op because it is already owned. Agreement, implementation, CI and merge are separate states. Retain failures and exact identities; do not sum overlapping spans or hide failure/time-out rates behind successful-task-only timings.
