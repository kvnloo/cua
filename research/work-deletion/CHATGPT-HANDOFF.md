## Safety gate: FAILED, GUI/input still paused

Normal headless Sway startup and basic private-route refusal probes worked, but the independent offline reviewer found five blockers:

1. Setup/cancellation exceptions and repeated signals can bypass or interrupt cleanup.
2. Environment-marker scans do not own descendants that scrub/filter that marker; PID identity races remain.
3. Symlinked endpoints and DBus address indirection can pass validation.
4. Fabricated leases are not bound to a genuine parent-owned Sway/Driver pair, endpoint peer or pinned executable.

The auditor used intercepted exec and owned scratch sockets; no live host escape was attempted or observed. Historical unchanged host focus is not proof that physical pointer/input was untouched. The native cursor probe was correctly delivery-only (`effect=unverifiable`), not a verified pointer-position oracle.
